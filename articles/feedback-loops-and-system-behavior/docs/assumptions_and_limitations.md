# Assumptions and Limitations

These examples use synthetic data and simplified feedback structures.

They are intended for learning, demonstration, institutional analysis, and reproducible article support. They should not be treated as validated models of real systems without empirical calibration, domain review, stakeholder critique, uncertainty analysis, and ethical assessment.

Key limitations:

- simplified variable sets
- assumed causal polarity
- synthetic indicator values
- simplified parameter values
- limited representation of power and institutions
- limited uncertainty modeling
- no direct stakeholder validation
