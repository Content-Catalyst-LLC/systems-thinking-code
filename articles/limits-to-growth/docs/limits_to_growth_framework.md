# Limits-to-Growth Framework

## Core structure

- Reinforcing loop: growth creates resources, confidence, legitimacy, or demand that enables more growth.
- Balancing loop: a constraint strengthens as growth consumes a supporting stock or approaches capacity.
- Delay: the constraint appears after commitments to growth have already accumulated.

## Leverage points

- Identify the constraint.
- Invest in supporting capacity.
- Reduce harmful throughput.
- Protect and regenerate supporting stocks.
- Change the system goal when growth depends on depletion.
