# Assumptions and Limitations

The workflows are synthetic demonstrations. They do not estimate real thresholds, certify resilience, or predict collapse. Actual resilience analysis requires domain data, expert review, stakeholder participation, uncertainty analysis, and context-specific governance.

Key limitations:

- Thresholds are simplified and rule-based.
- Pressure and resilience are represented as index values.
- Distributional vulnerability uses synthetic groups.
- Early warning diagnostics are illustrative.
- Results should be interpreted as methodological examples.
