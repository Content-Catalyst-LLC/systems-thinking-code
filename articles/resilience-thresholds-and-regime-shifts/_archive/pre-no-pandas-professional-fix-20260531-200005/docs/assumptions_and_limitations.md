# Assumptions and Limitations

This article repository uses synthetic data to demonstrate professional systems-analysis workflows for resilience, thresholds, and regime shifts. The scripts are designed to be runnable without third-party Python packages and with base R only, so they remain portable across local machines.

## Assumptions

- Resilience is represented as a bounded stock from 0 to 100.
- Pressure rises over time unless reduced by transformative intervention.
- Buffers, learning, adaptive capacity, and equity protection increase resilience or reduce vulnerability.
- Threshold risk is represented by the margin between resilience and pressure.
- Regime classification is a diagnostic aid, not a validated empirical prediction.

## Limitations

- Synthetic scenarios are not forecasts.
- Thresholds are simplified and should be replaced with domain-specific evidence.
- Social, ecological, institutional, and infrastructure systems require expert and community validation.
- Distributional vulnerability is simplified and should be expanded with real exposure, sensitivity, and adaptive-capacity data.
