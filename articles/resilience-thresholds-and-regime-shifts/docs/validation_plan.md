# Validation Plan

Because the workflows use synthetic data, validation focuses on reproducibility, internal consistency, and professional interpretability.

Recommended checks:

- Confirm all generated indicators remain in plausible bounds.
- Confirm threshold classifications follow documented rules.
- Confirm scenario summaries match time-series outputs.
- Confirm outputs are regenerated from source scripts without manual editing.
- Confirm charts and tables are labeled with synthetic-data notes.
- Confirm assumptions are reviewed before adapting the model to real data.
