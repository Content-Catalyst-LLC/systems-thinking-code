# Article Notes

Article: System Dynamics and Simulation Modeling

Core argument: system dynamics extends qualitative systems thinking into formal simulation by representing stocks, flows, feedback loops, delays, and scenarios. The goal is not certain prediction but structured learning about how system behavior emerges over time.

Key sections:

- Why system dynamics matters
- From systems thinking to system dynamics
- Stocks, flows, and feedback
- Why simulation is needed
- Model structure and behavior over time
- Delays, nonlinearity, and thresholds
- Scenarios, not simple predictions
- Model testing and validation
- Policy design and leverage
- Ethics and modeling responsibility
