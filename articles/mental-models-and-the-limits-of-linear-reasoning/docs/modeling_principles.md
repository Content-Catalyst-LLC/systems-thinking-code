# Modeling Principles

1. Treat every model as a partial representation, not reality itself.
2. Make assumptions, boundaries, causal claims, and exclusions explicit.
3. Compare linear explanations with feedback-aware explanations.
4. Include delays, stocks, flows, adaptation, and nonlinear response where relevant.
5. Use modeling to support inquiry, not to silence lived experience or affected communities.
6. Test models against behavior over time, not only isolated events.
