# Ethics, Control, and Accountability

Cybernetic systems involve information, control, regulation, and feedback. In human systems, these are ethical and political questions.

## Ethical requirements

- Make goals explicit.
- Identify who is controlled or monitored.
- Include affected voice.
- Provide contestability and appeal.
- Avoid surveillance disguised as learning.
- Track externalized harm.
- Build repair pathways.
- Ensure public accountability where public consequences exist.
