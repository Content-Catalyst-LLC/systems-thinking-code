# Cybernetics and General Systems Framework

This framework connects cybernetic feedback, general systems theory, and applied systems thinking.

## Core dimensions

- Feedback signals
- Reference goals
- Error correction
- Control action
- Disturbance variety
- Response variety
- Open-system exchange
- Boundaries
- Adaptation
- Learning
- Trust
- Accountability

## Diagnosis

A system should be diagnosed by the adequacy of its feedback, the legitimacy of its goals, its response variety, the openness of its boundary, and the accountability of its control structures.
