# Diagnostic Questions

1. What is the system boundary?
2. What is the system goal or reference state?
3. What feedback signals does the system receive?
4. Are feedback signals timely, trusted, and interpretable?
5. What control actions follow from feedback?
6. What disturbances must the system handle?
7. Does response variety match disturbance variety?
8. What crosses the system boundary?
9. What effects are externalized?
10. Where do delays create overshoot or oscillation?
11. Who is monitored and who controls the controller?
12. What accountability and repair mechanisms exist?
