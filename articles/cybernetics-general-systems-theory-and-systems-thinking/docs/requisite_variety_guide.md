# Requisite Variety Guide

Ashby’s law of requisite variety states that a regulator needs enough response variety to handle the variety of disturbances it faces.

## Practical questions

- What disturbances does the system face?
- How diverse are those disturbances?
- What responses are available?
- Who has authority to respond?
- What responses are missing?
- Does response variety exist at the right level?
- Are marginalized groups facing high disturbance variety with low response capacity?
