# Modeling Principles

This scaffold treats cybernetics and general systems theory as practical lenses for feedback, communication, control, open systems, variety, adaptation, and accountability.

## Principles

1. Identify the system goal before modeling regulation.
2. Model feedback quality and delay explicitly.
3. Compare disturbance variety with response variety.
4. Treat system boundaries as analytical and ethical choices.
5. Track open-system inputs, outputs, dependencies, and externalities.
6. Distinguish responsible regulation from domination or surveillance.
7. Include trust and accountability in public or organizational systems.
8. Use models for learning and critique, not unquestionable control.
