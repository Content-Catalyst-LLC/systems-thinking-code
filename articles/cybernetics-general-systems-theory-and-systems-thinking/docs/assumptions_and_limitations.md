# Assumptions and Limitations

This scaffold uses synthetic data and simplified formulas for demonstration.

## Limitations

- Synthetic data does not represent a real machine, organization, AI system, public agency, or ecosystem.
- The model is not a validated control, safety, AI governance, or public policy system.
- Requisite variety metrics are illustrative and should be debated.
- Social systems require qualitative evidence, participation, historical context, and ethical review.
- Model outputs should never override democratic judgment, professional review, or affected-community feedback.
