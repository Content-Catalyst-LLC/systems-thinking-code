# Article Notes

This article argues that the deepest systems interventions change the goals and paradigms that organize system behavior. Parameters and feedback matter, but the system's operating purpose determines what feedback is noticed, what metrics are rewarded, and what harms are normalized.

Core claims:

- Stated goals and operating goals often differ.
- Goals organize feedback.
- Paradigms define what counts as value, evidence, cost, harm, and possibility.
- Surface reform can preserve the old paradigm under new language.
- Deep change requires translating new goals into rules, metrics, resources, authority, and accountability.
