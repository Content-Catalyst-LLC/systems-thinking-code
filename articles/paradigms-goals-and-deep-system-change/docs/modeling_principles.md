# Modeling Principles

1. Treat goals and paradigms as system structures, not decorative language.
2. Distinguish stated goals from operating goals.
3. Test whether metrics reward the stated purpose or reproduce a hidden purpose.
4. Expand boundaries to include externalized costs and delayed harms.
5. Use synthetic data for demonstrations unless a real dataset has permission, governance, and context.
6. Evaluate distributional effects, not only aggregate outcomes.
7. Document assumptions and limitations clearly.
8. Treat models as structured reasoning tools, not automatic truth.
