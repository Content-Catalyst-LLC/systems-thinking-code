# Ethics and Power Notes

Paradigm and goal analysis must ask who defines the system's purpose. A goal can be efficient and unjust. A paradigm can be coherent and harmful. Deep system change must include affected people, externalized costs, long time horizons, and repair obligations.

Questions:

- Who benefits from the current operating goal?
- Who bears costs that the current boundary excludes?
- Who can challenge the system's metrics?
- What would the system measure if dignity, stewardship, or repair were central?
- What safeguards prevent the new paradigm from becoming coercive?
