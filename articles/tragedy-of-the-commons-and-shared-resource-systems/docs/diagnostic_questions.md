# Diagnostic Questions

1. What shared resource is being used?
2. Who depends on it?
3. Who benefits from additional use?
4. Who bears depletion costs?
5. Is total use above replenishment or repair capacity?
6. What feedback signals reveal depletion?
7. Are rules trusted and enforceable?
8. Who participates in governance?
9. Who is excluded from governance?
10. What restoration is required?
