# Ethics and Public Accountability

Intelligent infrastructure has ethical stakes because infrastructure determines access to water, energy, mobility, sanitation, cooling, public safety, health, and environmental protection.

## Key concerns

- Surveillance risk
- Unequal monitoring
- Unequal repair
- Vendor opacity
- Cybersecurity exposure
- Digital exclusion
- Environmental injustice
- Public trust
- Worker voice
- Community participation
- Right to reliable essential systems

Public infrastructure intelligence should deepen public responsibility rather than outsourcing accountability to dashboards or vendors.
