# Diagnostic Questions

1. What public function must this infrastructure system protect?
2. Which assets are most critical to safety, access, health, and continuity?
3. Where are the sensors, and what remains unmonitored?
4. What happens if the digital layer fails?
5. What happens if the physical asset fails?
6. Who receives maintenance first, and why?
7. Does the scoring system include vulnerable communities?
8. What vendors control essential data, models, or platforms?
9. Can workers and communities contest model outputs?
10. Does intelligence lead to repair, or only to visibility?
