# Modeling Principles

This scaffold treats intelligent infrastructure as a cyber-physical and institutional system. Models should connect asset condition, sensor reliability, maintenance capacity, climate exposure, cyber-physical dependency, workforce capacity, governance readiness, equity priority, and public accountability.

## Principles

1. Model infrastructure as a system, not as isolated assets.
2. Combine probability of failure with consequence of failure.
3. Include maintenance capacity, not only sensing.
4. Treat cyber-physical dependency as infrastructure risk.
5. Include equity, public trust, and service criticality.
6. Keep assumptions visible and testable.
7. Distinguish synthetic demonstration from operational deployment.
8. Preserve human engineering review and public accountability.
