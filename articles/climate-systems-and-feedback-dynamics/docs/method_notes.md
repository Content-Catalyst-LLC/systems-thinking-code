# Method Notes

The workflow demonstrates climate-system concepts through a transparent synthetic model:

1. emissions as flows;
2. atmospheric CO2 as a stock;
3. radiative forcing as a function of concentration;
4. feedback multipliers as simplified amplifiers;
5. ocean heat uptake as a delay/storage term;
6. vulnerability and adaptation as social-system modifiers of risk;
7. policy timing as a determinant of cumulative emissions.

The purpose is to make systems relationships legible, not to forecast the climate.
