# Responsible Use

These scripts are for synthetic-data research, education, scenario reasoning, reproducibility demonstration, and systems-method documentation.

Do not use these scripts as the sole basis for:

- engineering design,
- insurance pricing,
- public-safety decisions,
- regulatory compliance,
- financial risk disclosure,
- site-specific climate adaptation,
- legal claims,
- infrastructure investment decisions.

For professional climate-risk work, use validated climate datasets, peer-reviewed methods, uncertainty analysis, local context, affected-community input, and qualified domain review.
