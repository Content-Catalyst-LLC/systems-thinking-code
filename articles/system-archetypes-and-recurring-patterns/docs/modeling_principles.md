# Modeling Principles

System archetype modeling should begin with behavior over time, not with labels. Treat each archetype as a diagnostic hypothesis that must be tested against evidence, stakeholder experience, causal structure, and historical context.

Principles:

1. Start with the recurring pattern.
2. Identify the symptom and the system response.
3. Map feedback loops before choosing an intervention.
4. Look for delayed consequences.
5. Identify stocks that accumulate or deplete.
6. Ask who benefits from recurrence and who bears the cost.
7. Treat model results as structured reasoning, not final truth.
8. Document assumptions and limitations.
