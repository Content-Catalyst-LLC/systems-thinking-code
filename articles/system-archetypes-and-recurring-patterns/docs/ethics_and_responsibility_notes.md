# Ethics and Responsibility Notes

Archetype analysis should reveal structure without dissolving responsibility. Recurring harm is not automatically nobody's fault. Structures are designed, funded, maintained, defended, and changed by people and institutions.

Responsible use requires attention to:

- Blame avoidance: do not blame individuals for behavior produced by structural constraints.
- Accountability: do not use “the system” as an excuse for institutional inaction.
- Distribution: examine who benefits and who bears delayed costs.
- Boundary critique: identify harms shifted outside official metrics.
- Repair: connect diagnosis to structural intervention and responsibility.
