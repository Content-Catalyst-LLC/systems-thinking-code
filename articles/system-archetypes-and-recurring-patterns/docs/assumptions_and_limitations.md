# Assumptions and Limitations

This scaffold uses synthetic data and simplified models. The examples are intended for explanation, teaching, and reproducible systems-thinking workflows.

Limitations:

- Synthetic data do not represent a real community, institution, ecosystem, or population.
- Archetype labels are diagnostic hypotheses, not final explanations.
- Real systems may contain multiple overlapping archetypes.
- Quantitative simulations simplify historical, legal, cultural, ecological, and political context.
- Any real-world use requires domain validation and stakeholder review.
