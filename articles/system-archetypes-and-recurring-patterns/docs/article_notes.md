# Article Notes

This scaffold supports the article “System Archetypes and Recurring Patterns.” The article explains archetypes as recurring feedback structures that generate repeated behavior across domains.

Core archetypes represented in the examples:

- Limits to Growth
- Fixes That Fail
- Shifting the Burden
- Eroding Goals
- Escalation
- Success to the Successful
- Tragedy of the Commons
- Growth and Underinvestment
- Policy Resistance
