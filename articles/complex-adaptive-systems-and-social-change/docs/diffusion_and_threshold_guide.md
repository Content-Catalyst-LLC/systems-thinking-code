# Diffusion and Threshold Guide

Social adoption often spreads through networks rather than through direct information transfer. Thresholds represent the level of visible adoption, trust, or legitimacy required before an agent changes behavior.

## Useful questions

- Which agents are early adopters?
- Which agents are bridge nodes?
- What adoption threshold does each group require?
- Where is trust high or low?
- What communities are isolated from diffusion?
- Which channels create legitimacy?
- What forms of backlash slow or redirect adoption?
