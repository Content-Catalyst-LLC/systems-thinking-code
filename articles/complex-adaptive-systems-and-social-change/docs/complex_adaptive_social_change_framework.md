# Complex Adaptive Social Change Framework

Complex social change emerges through interacting agents, institutions, networks, rules, narratives, technologies, resources, resistance, and feedback loops.

## Core dimensions

- Adaptive agents
- Network diffusion
- Threshold dynamics
- Trust and legitimacy
- Resistance and backlash
- Institutional response
- Coalition capacity
- Governance learning
- Historical memory
- Equity alignment
- Transformation momentum

## Diagnosis

A change process becomes more durable when adoption, legitimacy, institutional response, learning capacity, participation quality, and equity alignment grow faster than resistance and backlash.
