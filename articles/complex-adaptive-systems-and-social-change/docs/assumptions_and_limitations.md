# Assumptions and Limitations

This scaffold uses synthetic data and simplified formulas for demonstration.

## Limitations

- Synthetic data does not represent a real community, institution, or movement.
- Social change cannot be reduced to numeric indicators alone.
- Models must be interpreted with qualitative evidence and affected-community knowledge.
- Power, history, meaning, and legitimacy require contextual analysis.
- Outputs should not be used to rank real communities or predict political behavior.
