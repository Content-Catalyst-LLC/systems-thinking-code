# Modeling Principles

This scaffold treats social change as a complex adaptive process rather than a linear intervention pipeline.

## Principles

1. Model agents as adaptive, not passive.
2. Track trust, legitimacy, resistance, and learning over time.
3. Include institutions and counter-adaptation.
4. Treat social networks as diffusion structures.
5. Distinguish visibility from transformation.
6. Include equity and burden of adaptation.
7. Make assumptions visible and testable.
8. Use models to support judgment, not replace participatory analysis.
