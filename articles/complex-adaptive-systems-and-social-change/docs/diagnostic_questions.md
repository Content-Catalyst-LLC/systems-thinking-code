# Diagnostic Questions

1. What system is being changed?
2. Who are the adaptive agents?
3. What formal and informal rules shape behavior?
4. What feedback loops reinforce the current pattern?
5. What networks allow diffusion?
6. Where are thresholds near or far?
7. Who benefits from the status quo?
8. How might opponents counter-adapt?
9. Whose knowledge is included in feedback?
10. Who bears the burden of adaptation?
11. What would durable transformation change structurally?
12. What institutional memory must be preserved for accountability?
