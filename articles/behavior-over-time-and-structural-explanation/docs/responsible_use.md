# Responsible Use

This scaffold is for synthetic-data analysis, educational demonstration, and systems-thinking method development.

Do not use these examples to automate decisions about people, communities, infrastructure, public benefits, risk classification, employment, education, policing, lending, insurance, medical care, or environmental harm.

Responsible systems analysis should include domain expertise, stakeholder participation, governance review, documentation of uncertainty, and attention to unequal distribution of harms and benefits.
