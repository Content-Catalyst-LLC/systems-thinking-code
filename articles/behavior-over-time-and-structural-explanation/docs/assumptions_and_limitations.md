# Assumptions and Limitations

The datasets are synthetic and intentionally simplified. They are designed to demonstrate systems-thinking workflows, not to represent real organizations, governments, ecosystems, or communities.

Limitations include:

- simplified variable definitions;
- invented causal relationships;
- no real-world validation;
- limited representation of power, governance, history, and lived experience;
- no claim of predictive accuracy.

Use these examples as templates for thinking, documentation, and reproducible analysis.
