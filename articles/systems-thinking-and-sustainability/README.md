# Systems Thinking and Sustainability

Companion scaffold for the Systems Thinking article **Systems Thinking and Sustainability**.

This repository models sustainability as a dynamic system of ecological limits, social foundations, stocks, flows, feedback loops, delays, thresholds, resilience, justice, governance, and institutional learning.

## Featured workflows

- **Python Workflow: Sustainability Feedback, Stock-Flow, and Scenario Modeling**
- **R Workflow: Indicator Visualization, Sensitivity Tables, and Sustainability Diagnostics**

## Responsible use

Synthetic-data educational scaffold only. Do not use as an operational environmental, infrastructure, legal, investment, or public-policy decision system without domain expertise, transparent assumptions, participatory review, and real data validation.
