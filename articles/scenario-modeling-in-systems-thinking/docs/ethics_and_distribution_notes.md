# Ethics and Distribution Notes

Scenario modeling shapes future imagination. Responsible scenario work should ask:

- Who defines the scenario set?
- Whose assumptions drive the model?
- Which groups are included or excluded?
- What burdens are shifted across place or time?
- What future harms are foreseeable?
- What repair obligations follow from foreseeable risk?
- What monitoring signals should trigger intervention?

Aggregate outcomes should not be treated as sufficient when the system distributes benefits and burdens unequally.
