# Systems Modeling Principles

## 1. Start with behavior over time
A system model should explain recurring behavior, not just name components.

## 2. Define the boundary
Every system boundary includes and excludes. Boundary choices are analytical and ethical choices.

## 3. Identify stocks and flows
Stocks explain memory, accumulation, depletion, capacity, and delay.

## 4. Make feedback explicit
A systems claim should identify reinforcing or balancing relationships where possible.

## 5. Treat delay seriously
Delays often explain oscillation, overcorrection, policy resistance, and mislearning.

## 6. Use scenarios, not single forecasts
Systems analysis should compare plausible pathways under different assumptions.

## 7. Distinguish leverage from pressure
More effort at the wrong level does not equal structural change.

## 8. Preserve humility
A model is a disciplined representation, not reality itself.
