# Systems Thinking: Patterns, Interdependence, and Structural Change

This folder supports the Systems Thinking pillar with reproducible examples for:

- causal-loop diagrams
- reinforcing and balancing feedback
- delay and oscillation
- stock-and-flow behavior
- leverage-point scenarios
- system archetypes
- resilience and threshold behavior
- network dependency and cascade risk
- SQL system-model metadata

Important distinction: systems models clarify structural hypotheses. They do not automatically establish causality, justice, or policy legitimacy.
