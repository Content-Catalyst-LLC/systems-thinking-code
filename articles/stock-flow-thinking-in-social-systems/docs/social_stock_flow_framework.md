# Social Stock-Flow Framework

A social stock is an accumulated condition that affects present and future behavior. A social flow is a rate that builds or drains that condition.

## Social Stocks

- Trust
- Legitimacy
- Wealth
- Debt
- Capability
- Administrative burden
- Institutional capacity
- Social cohesion
- Public health burden
- Climate vulnerability

## Social Flows

- Reliable service
- Harm and exclusion
- Income and debt service
- Investment and disinvestment
- Learning and forgetting
- Hiring and turnover
- Simplification and procedural friction
- Repair and retraumatization

## Repair Logic

A depleted social stock recovers only when repair inflows exceed ongoing depletion outflows over time.
