# Assumptions and Limitations

This scaffold uses synthetic data and simplified equations to demonstrate stock-flow thinking in social systems.

## Assumptions

- Social conditions can be represented as accumulated stocks for analytic purposes.
- Inflows and outflows can be estimated or simulated.
- Feedback can compound advantage or disadvantage.
- Repair requires sustained positive net flow into depleted stocks.

## Limitations

- Synthetic data cannot support empirical claims.
- Social stocks such as trust, legitimacy, and capability cannot be fully captured by numeric proxies.
- Group-level examples should never be used to classify individuals.
- Models require historical interpretation, stakeholder review, and ethical judgment.
