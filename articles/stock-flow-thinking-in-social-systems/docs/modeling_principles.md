# Modeling Principles

Stock-flow thinking in social systems should clarify accumulation, depletion, repair, and distribution. The goal is not to reduce social life to numbers, but to make structural assumptions explicit.

## Principles

1. Begin with a social behavior-over-time pattern.
2. Identify the social stock that stores history.
3. Separate inflows from outflows.
4. Ask who receives the inflows and who bears the outflows.
5. Include feedback where stocks shape future flows.
6. Treat trust, burden, legitimacy, and capability as historically produced conditions.
7. Use scenarios to examine repair, not only prediction.
8. Pair quantitative examples with qualitative interpretation and stakeholder knowledge.

## Key Questions

- What is accumulating?
- What is depleting?
- Who controls the flows?
- What harms are hidden by official metrics?
- What repair flows are required?
- What time horizon is needed for stock recovery?
