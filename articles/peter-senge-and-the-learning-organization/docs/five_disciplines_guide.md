# Five Disciplines Guide

## Personal mastery

Human development, purpose, skill growth, and the creative tension between vision and current reality.

## Mental models

The assumptions and causal stories that shape how people interpret evidence.

## Shared vision

A genuinely owned purpose that is structurally supported by metrics, budget, and decisions.

## Team learning

Collective inquiry, dialogue, cross-boundary thinking, and coordinated intelligence.

## Systems thinking

The discipline that integrates the others by identifying feedback structures and recurring patterns.
