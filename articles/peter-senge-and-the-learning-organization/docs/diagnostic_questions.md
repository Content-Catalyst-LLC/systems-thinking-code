# Diagnostic Questions

1. What recurring learning failure is visible?
2. What feedback is being collected but not used?
3. Where is psychological safety low?
4. What assumptions are treated as facts?
5. What shared vision is stated but not structurally supported?
6. What teams need to learn across boundaries?
7. What defensive routines suppress truth?
8. What knowledge is lost through turnover or fragmentation?
9. What incentives reward local optimization?
10. Who has authority to act on feedback?
11. What larger systems does the organization affect?
12. What would ethical organizational learning require?
