# Defensive Routines Notes

Defensive routines protect people and institutions from embarrassment, threat, blame, or loss of control, but they suppress learning.

## Common routines

- Blame shifting
- Bad-news filtering
- Metric theater
- Silo defense
- Shallow agreement
- Excessive process
- Avoidance of conflict
- Ritualized consultation without change

## Diagnostic rule

If feedback repeatedly fails to change decisions, look for defensive routines and the structures that reward them.
