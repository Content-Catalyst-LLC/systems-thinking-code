# Diagnostic Questions

1. What behavior over time needs explanation?
2. What stocks preserve the system’s history?
3. What flows increase or decrease each stock?
4. What feedback loops reinforce the current pattern?
5. What loops balance or constrain change?
6. Where are perception, decision, implementation, and learning delays?
7. What decision rules govern action?
8. What policy resistance is likely?
9. What leverage points change structure rather than symptoms?
10. Who should participate in boundary critique and model review?
