# Ethics and Modeling Accountability

System dynamics models can influence real decisions. When models affect public systems, they require transparency, participation, boundary critique, and accountability.

## Ethical requirements

- Make assumptions visible.
- Communicate uncertainty and sensitivity.
- Include affected knowledge.
- Do not convert contested values into technical inevitability.
- Identify who benefits and who bears risk.
- Treat models as learning tools, not as unquestionable authority.
