# Stocks, Flows, and Delays Guide

## Stocks

Stocks are accumulations that preserve history: backlog, trust, capacity, debt, pollution, housing, skill, inventory, or institutional memory.

## Flows

Flows increase or decrease stocks: repairs, hiring, emissions, learning, attrition, investment, spending, migration, or service delivery.

## Delays

Delays separate action from effect. They can produce overshoot, oscillation, false confidence, or premature abandonment of effective interventions.
