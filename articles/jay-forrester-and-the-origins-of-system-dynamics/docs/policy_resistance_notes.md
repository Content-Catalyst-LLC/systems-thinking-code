# Policy Resistance Notes

Policy resistance occurs when a system responds to intervention in ways that weaken, offset, or reverse the intended effect.

## Sources

- Delayed perception
- Delayed implementation
- Compensating feedback
- Resource constraints
- Institutional routines
- Low trust
- Misaligned incentives
- Local optimization
- Counter-adaptation

## Diagnostic rule

When a policy repeatedly fails, ask what feedback loop is preserving the unwanted behavior.
