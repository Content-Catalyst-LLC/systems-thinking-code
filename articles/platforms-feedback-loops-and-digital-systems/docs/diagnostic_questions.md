# Diagnostic Questions for Platform Systems

- What behavior does the platform reward?
- What signals shape ranking, recommendation, and visibility?
- What feedback loops connect user behavior, data, ranking, exposure, and future behavior?
- What harms are amplified rather than merely hosted?
- Who depends on the platform for income, communication, identity, or access?
- What moderation labor is required, and who performs it?
- What appeal, contestability, transparency, audit, and remedy mechanisms exist?
- What would reduce harmful cascades without suppressing legitimate expression or public-interest organizing?
