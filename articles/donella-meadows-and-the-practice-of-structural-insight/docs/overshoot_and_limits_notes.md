# Overshoot and Limits Notes

Overshoot occurs when growth continues beyond regenerative capacity because feedback from limits is delayed, ignored, weakened, or politically suppressed.

## Warning signs

- Resource stock depletion
- Delayed visible harm
- Consumption faster than regeneration
- Weak or hidden feedback
- Political denial of limits
- Efficiency gains used to increase throughput
- Unequal distribution of harm

Sustainability requires that flows remain within regenerative and absorptive limits over time.
