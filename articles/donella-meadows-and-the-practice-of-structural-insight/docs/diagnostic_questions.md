# Diagnostic Questions

1. What recurring pattern needs explanation?
2. What stock preserves the system’s history?
3. What inflows and outflows change the stock?
4. Where is feedback delayed, hidden, or suppressed?
5. Which loops reinforce extraction, distrust, backlog, pollution, or inequity?
6. Which balancing loops are too weak or delayed?
7. Who receives information and who can act on it?
8. What rules reward harmful behavior?
9. What is the system actually optimizing?
10. What paradigm makes the current system appear normal?
11. Who bears the burden of adaptation?
12. What leverage point changes structure rather than symptoms?
