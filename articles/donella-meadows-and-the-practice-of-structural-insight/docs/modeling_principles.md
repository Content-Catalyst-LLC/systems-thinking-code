# Modeling Principles

This scaffold treats structural insight as a disciplined practice of moving from events to patterns, from patterns to feedback structure, and from feedback structure to leverage.

## Principles

1. Identify stocks before proposing interventions.
2. Track inflows and outflows over time.
3. Represent delayed feedback explicitly.
4. Distinguish low-leverage parameter changes from deeper rule, goal, and paradigm shifts.
5. Treat resilience as an ethical question: resilience of what, for whom, and toward what purpose?
6. Include equity and the burden of adaptation.
7. Use models to support learning, not certainty.
8. Connect information flows to accountability and remedy.
