# Meadows Structural Insight Framework

Donella Meadows’s work helps analysts see why systems produce recurring patterns and where meaningful change may be possible.

## Core dimensions

- Stocks and flows
- Feedback loops
- Delays
- Overshoot and limits
- Resilience and buffers
- Information flows
- Rules and incentives
- Self-organization
- Goals
- Paradigms
- Equity and repair
- Public responsibility

## Diagnosis

A system should be diagnosed by the patterns it reproduces, the stocks it accumulates, the feedback loops it amplifies, and the goals it actually serves.
