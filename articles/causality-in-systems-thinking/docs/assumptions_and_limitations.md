# Assumptions and Limitations

These examples use synthetic data and simplified causal structures.

They are intended for learning, demonstration, and reproducible article support. They should not be treated as final causal models of real-world systems without domain validation, empirical calibration, stakeholder review, uncertainty analysis, and ethical review.

Key limitations:

- synthetic data
- simplified variable sets
- assumed causal direction
- limited parameter calibration
- simplified threshold behavior
- no real-world causal identification claims
- no direct stakeholder validation

Good causal modeling should clarify uncertainty rather than hide it.
