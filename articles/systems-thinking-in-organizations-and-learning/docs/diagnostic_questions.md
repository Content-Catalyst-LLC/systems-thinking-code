# Diagnostic Questions

- What problem keeps recurring?
- What stock is accumulating or depleting?
- Where does feedback become delayed, filtered, distorted, or punished?
- What incentives encourage local optimization?
- What assumptions define the problem?
- Whose feedback is trusted, ignored, or punished?
- What knowledge leaves when people leave?
- Is burnout treated as individual weakness or system feedback?
- Does learning change structure, or only produce documents?
