# Assumptions and Limitations

The workflows use synthetic data and simplified equations. They are not epidemiological forecasts, clinical decision tools, outbreak projections, or public-health policy recommendations.

Use these scripts to:

- structure scenario analysis;
- document assumptions;
- compare intervention timing and care-capacity effects;
- explore public trust and vulnerability dynamics;
- prototype diagnostics before replacing synthetic assumptions with observed data.

Do not use the generated outputs as empirical claims without replacing inputs, validating assumptions, consulting public-health experts, and reviewing ethical implications.
