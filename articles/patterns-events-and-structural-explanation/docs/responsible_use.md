# Responsible Use

Systems models can shape decisions, priorities, and narratives.

Use these materials responsibly:

- Do not treat synthetic examples as empirical proof.
- Do not use simplified structural models to blame communities, workers, students, patients, or other affected groups.
- Document assumptions, boundaries, and exclusions.
- Ask who benefits, who bears risk, and whose knowledge is missing.
- Treat model outputs as prompts for inquiry, not automatic answers.
