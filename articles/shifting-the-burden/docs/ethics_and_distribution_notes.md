# Ethics and Distribution Notes

Shifting the burden is not only a technical pattern. It is a justice issue. When institutions manage symptoms without repair, unresolved costs often move to less powerful groups.

## Ethical risks

- Administrative burden shifted to applicants and families
- Workload pressure shifted to frontline workers
- Infrastructure risk shifted to residents and future budgets
- Climate risk shifted to vulnerable communities and future generations
- AI contestation burden shifted to users
- Health crisis burden shifted to emergency departments, patients, and caregivers

## Responsible-use principles

- Count externalized burden.
- Include affected people in defining the fundamental problem.
- Treat relief as temporary stabilization, not proof of repair.
- Track dependency reduction as a success metric.
- Design transition paths that avoid abrupt withdrawal of necessary support.
