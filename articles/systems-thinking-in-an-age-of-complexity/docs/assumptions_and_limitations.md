# Assumptions and Limitations

This scaffold uses synthetic data and simplified formulas for demonstration.

## Limitations

- Synthetic data does not represent a real city, platform, institution, infrastructure system, ecosystem, or community.
- Scores are illustrative and should not be used for certification or operational decisions.
- Complexity, resilience, accountability, and transformation require qualitative evidence and professional judgment.
- Model outputs should support deliberation, not replace public participation, ecological review, or affected-community knowledge.
