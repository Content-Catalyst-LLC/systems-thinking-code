# Complexity Pressure Guide

Complexity pressure increases when systems are highly interdependent, feedback-rich, delayed, adaptive, and uncertain.

## Practical questions

- Which systems depend on one another?
- Which loops amplify or stabilize behavior?
- Where do delays hide consequences?
- Who adapts and how?
- What uncertainty cannot be resolved before action?
- What response capacity exists?
- What harm accumulates while institutions wait?
