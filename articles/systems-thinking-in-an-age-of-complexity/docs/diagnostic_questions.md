# Diagnostic Questions

1. What recurring pattern defines the problem?
2. What complexity pressures are present?
3. What is accumulating or depleting?
4. What feedback loops reinforce the pattern?
5. Where are delays hiding consequences?
6. What boundary excludes harm or affected knowledge?
7. Where is the system brittle despite apparent efficiency?
8. What resilience capacity exists?
9. What institutions can learn and revise?
10. Who benefits from current structure?
11. What harm is accumulating and who bears it?
12. What leverage changes goals, rules, information, or power?
