# Modeling Principles

This scaffold treats ethical systems thinking as a practical discipline for boundary critique, accountability, repair, ecological responsibility, and power-aware systems intervention.

## Principles

1. Make boundaries explicit and contestable.
2. Count consequences experienced, not only consequences measured.
3. Include affected voice as part of system knowledge.
4. Track harm exposure and cumulative burden.
5. Distinguish efficiency from ethical value.
6. Model repair capacity, not only performance.
7. Treat ecological systems as inside the moral field.
8. Use models for deliberation, not as unquestionable authority.
