# Diagnostic Questions

1. What does the system claim to optimize?
2. What does it actually reward?
3. Who framed the problem?
4. What boundary was drawn?
5. Who is outside the model but inside the consequences?
6. What harms accumulate over time?
7. Who benefits from the current structure?
8. Who can safely contest decisions?
9. What ecological dependencies are externalized?
10. What model assumptions hide power?
11. What repair is owed?
12. What leverage point changes structure and power?
