# Assumptions and Limitations

This scaffold uses synthetic data and simplified formulas for demonstration.

## Limitations

- Synthetic data does not represent a real institution, community, workplace, platform, policy, or ecosystem.
- Scores are illustrative and should not be used for certification or compliance.
- Ethical analysis cannot be reduced to an index.
- Lived experience, qualitative evidence, legal context, ecological knowledge, and affected voice are essential.
- Model outputs should support deliberation, not replace it.
