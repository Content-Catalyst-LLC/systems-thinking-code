# Ethics Systems Framework

Ethical systems thinking links structural insight with accountability, participation, repair, and ecological responsibility.

## Core dimensions

- Boundary inclusion
- Affected voice
- Transparency
- Contestability
- Remedy and repair
- Oversight
- Harm exposure
- Ecological responsibility
- Model humility
- Structural change
- Power redistribution

## Diagnosis

A system should be diagnosed by who it includes, who it harms, who can contest it, what it repairs, what it externalizes, and whether it changes the structures that reproduce harm.
