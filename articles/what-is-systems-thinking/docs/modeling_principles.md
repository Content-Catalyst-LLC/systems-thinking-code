# Modeling Principles

## 1. Start with behavior over time

Systems thinking begins with recurring patterns, not isolated events. Model behavior over time before proposing solutions.

## 2. Distinguish stocks from flows

Stocks accumulate. Flows change stocks. Many systems are misunderstood because analysts track rates while ignoring accumulation.

## 3. Make feedback explicit

A systems model should identify how consequences return to influence causes.

## 4. Represent delay

Delayed consequences often explain overshoot, instability, policy resistance, and slow recovery.

## 5. Treat boundaries as analytical choices

Every model includes some relationships and excludes others. Document the boundary.

## 6. Avoid false precision

A model can clarify assumptions without pretending to capture the whole system.

## 7. Connect modeling to ethics

Ask who benefits, who bears risk, which costs are externalized, and whose experience is missing.
