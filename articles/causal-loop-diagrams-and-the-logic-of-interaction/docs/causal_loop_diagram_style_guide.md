# Causal Loop Diagram Style Guide

## Variable naming

Use clear, directional variables:

- Public Trust
- Staffing Capacity
- Response Delay
- Maintenance Backlog
- Administrative Burden
- Ecological Resilience

Avoid vague topics:

- Policy
- Technology
- Culture
- Problems
- People

## Link polarity

- Positive means same-direction movement.
- Negative means opposite-direction movement.
- Neither sign means good or bad.

## Delays

Mark delays separately from causal signs. A delayed link can be positive or negative.

## Boundaries

Document what the map includes and excludes. Consider whether the boundary hides costs, harms, affected groups, ecological effects, or future consequences.
