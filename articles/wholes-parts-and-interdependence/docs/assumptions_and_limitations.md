# Assumptions and Limitations

These examples use synthetic data and simplified system relationships.

They are intended for learning, demonstration, and reproducible article support. They should not be treated as final models of real-world systems without domain validation, stakeholder review, empirical calibration, and uncertainty analysis.

Key limitations:

- simplified dependency structures
- assumed relationship direction
- synthetic indicator data
- simplified failure propagation logic
- no empirical calibration
- no direct stakeholder validation
- no claim of predictive completeness

The models are designed to clarify part-whole reasoning, not to replace domain expertise or governance judgment.
