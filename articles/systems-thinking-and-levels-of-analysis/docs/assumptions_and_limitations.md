# Assumptions and Limitations

These materials use simplified synthetic data.

They are not empirical findings about any real organization, community, infrastructure system, or ecological system.

Limitations:

- synthetic entities and indicators
- simplified causal structure
- illustrative cross-level relationships
- no empirical calibration
- no direct stakeholder validation
- no claim of predictive completeness

Use these examples to learn structure, not to make operational decisions without domain-specific evidence and review.
