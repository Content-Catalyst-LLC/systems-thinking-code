# Ethics and Emergent Harm

Emergent harm can appear without one actor intending it. That does not remove responsibility. It shifts the analysis toward rules, incentives, ownership, governance, history, and power.

Use these workflows to ask:

- Who benefits from the emergent pattern?
- Who is harmed by it?
- Who has authority to change the rules?
- What feedback has been ignored?
- What repair is owed when harm becomes visible?
