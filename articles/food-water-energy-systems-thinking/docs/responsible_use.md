# Responsible Use

Food, water, and energy systems affect essential needs and public rights. Use this scaffold responsibly:

- Do not use synthetic outputs as real evidence.
- Do not make allocation decisions without validated data and affected-community participation.
- Document assumptions, uncertainty, exclusions, and trade-offs.
- Include equity, affordability, rights, and ecological integrity in model review.
- Treat model results as decision support, not decision replacement.
