# Modeling Principles

This scaffold models the food-water-energy nexus as a dynamic system of stocks, flows, feedback loops, trade-offs, and resilience indicators.

Professional use should follow these principles:

1. Treat groundwater, soil health, infrastructure condition, public trust, and resilience as stocks.
2. Treat withdrawals, recharge, pumping energy, production, emissions, and food movement as flows.
3. Track cross-sector dependencies rather than optimizing one sector alone.
4. Report uncertainty and assumptions explicitly.
5. Include equity, affordability, and vulnerability in resource-security analysis.
6. Validate synthetic scenarios before substituting real data.
