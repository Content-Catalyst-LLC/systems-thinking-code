# Diagnostic Questions

- Is workload exceeding capacity over time?
- What labor is invisible in official metrics?
- Who absorbs emotional labor, rework, and urgent requests?
- Is turnover draining institutional memory?
- Does urgency create errors and future workload?
- Are high performers receiving more work because they are reliable?
- Does the organization praise sacrifice instead of reducing the need for it?
- What structural redesign would reduce dependency on hidden labor?
