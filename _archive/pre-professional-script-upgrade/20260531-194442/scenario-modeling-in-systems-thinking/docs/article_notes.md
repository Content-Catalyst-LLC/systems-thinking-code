# Article Notes

The article argues that scenario modeling helps systems thinkers compare possible futures without pretending the future is predictable. Scenarios are not forecasts; they are structured possibilities grounded in assumptions, feedback loops, stocks, flows, delays, constraints, and interventions.

Key article concepts:

- scenarios versus predictions
- baseline continuation
- counterfactual reasoning
- drivers and uncertainties
- feedback and accumulation
- intervention pathways
- stress testing and resilience
- distributional outcomes
- ethics of future imagination
