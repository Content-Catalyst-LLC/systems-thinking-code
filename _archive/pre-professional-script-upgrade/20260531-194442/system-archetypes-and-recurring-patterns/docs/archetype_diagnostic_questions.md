# Archetype Diagnostic Questions

## Limits to Growth
- What reinforcing process is driving growth?
- What constraint is emerging?
- Is the constraint delayed or hidden?

## Fixes That Fail
- What short-term fix reduces pressure?
- What delayed consequence does it create?
- Who bears the delayed cost?

## Shifting the Burden
- What symptomatic solution is substituting for fundamental repair?
- Is dependence on the symptomatic solution growing?
- What fundamental capacity is being weakened?

## Eroding Goals
- What goal is being lowered?
- What pressure makes goal erosion attractive?
- Who is harmed by reduced expectations?

## Escalation
- What relative comparison drives intensification?
- What shared goal or rule could interrupt the cycle?

## Success to the Successful
- How are resources allocated toward prior success?
- What capacity-building mechanism would reduce cumulative disadvantage?

## Tragedy of the Commons
- What shared stock is being depleted?
- Are private gains separated from collective costs?
