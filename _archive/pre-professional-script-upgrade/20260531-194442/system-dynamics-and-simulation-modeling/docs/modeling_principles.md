# Modeling Principles

System dynamics modeling is a disciplined way to connect structure with behavior over time. The purpose is not to create certainty, but to make assumptions visible and testable.

## Principles

1. Begin with behavior over time.
2. Identify stocks before flows.
3. Separate inflows and outflows.
4. Represent feedback explicitly.
5. Document delays and nonlinearities.
6. Compare scenarios rather than relying on one forecast.
7. Test sensitivity to uncertain assumptions.
8. Treat models as learning tools, not automatic authority.

## Modeling Questions

- What stock is accumulating or depleting?
- What flows change that stock?
- What feedback controls those flows?
- What delays separate action from effect?
- Which assumptions drive the results?
- Who benefits or bears costs in each scenario?
