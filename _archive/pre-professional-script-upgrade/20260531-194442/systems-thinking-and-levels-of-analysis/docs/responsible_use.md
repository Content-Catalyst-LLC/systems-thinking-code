# Responsible Use

Level-of-analysis work can affect how blame, accountability, risk, and intervention are assigned.

Use these materials responsibly:

- Do not blame individuals for outcomes generated by organizational, institutional, historical, or ecological structures.
- Do not hide local harm inside high-level averages.
- Do not use cross-scale complexity to evade responsibility.
- Document the level of each claim.
- Include affected communities and frontline knowledge where real systems are being analyzed.
