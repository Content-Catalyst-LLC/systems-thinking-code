# Responsible Use

Causal explanations influence blame, policy, intervention, resource allocation, and accountability.

Use these materials responsibly:

- Do not treat synthetic examples as empirical proof.
- Do not use simplified causal models to override lived experience or domain expertise.
- Document assumptions, boundaries, and excluded variables.
- Distinguish correlation from causal mechanism.
- Consider who is blamed or protected by the causal explanation.
- Treat model outputs as prompts for inquiry, not automatic answers.
