# Responsible Use

This scaffold is intended for education, conceptual modeling, and reproducible systems-thinking demonstrations.

Do not use these examples for:

- individual-level assessment;
- employee monitoring or workforce surveillance;
- hiring, promotion, compensation, discipline, or termination;
- public-benefits eligibility or automated public-administration decisions;
- clinical triage or health decision-making;
- predictive policing or enforcement targeting;
- automated credit, housing, insurance, or legal determinations.

Use this material to support structural reasoning, transparency, documentation, and accountable discussion.
