# Article Notes

The article argues that shifting the burden occurs when a system manages symptoms while neglecting or weakening the fundamental solution. The visible problem appears to improve, but underlying capacity remains weak or declines.

## Article themes

- Symptomatic relief versus fundamental repair
- Dependency growth
- Capacity erosion
- Institutional learning failure
- Externalized burden
- Relief plus repair
- Ethical responsibility for shifted costs

## Related systems examples

- Overtime substituting for staffing and workload redesign
- Emergency care substituting for prevention and primary care
- Messaging substituting for trust repair
- Automation substituting for rule simplification and accountability
- Debt substituting for income security
- Technical mitigation substituting for ecological repair
