# Diagnostic Questions

## Symptom

- What visible problem keeps returning?
- Who experiences the symptom most directly?
- What official metric captures the symptom?
- What unofficial suffering or labor is not captured?

## Symptomatic solution

- What response reduces pressure quickly?
- Who is rewarded for applying it?
- What does it make easier for the institution?
- What does it make harder for affected people?

## Fundamental solution

- What would reduce the underlying cause?
- Why has it not been funded, authorized, or prioritized?
- What capacity must be rebuilt?
- What time horizon is required?

## Dependency

- Is reliance on the symptomatic solution increasing?
- Are budgets, tools, roles, and identities forming around relief?
- Is the system becoming less able to function without the fix?

## Burden

- Who carries the hidden work?
- Whose time, health, trust, money, ecosystem, or future is depleted?
- What would change if those burdens were counted?
