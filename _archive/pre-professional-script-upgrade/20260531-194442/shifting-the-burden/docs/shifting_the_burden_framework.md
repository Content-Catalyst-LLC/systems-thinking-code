# Shifting the Burden Framework

## Diagnostic structure

A shifting-the-burden pattern includes:

- a recurring symptom;
- a symptomatic solution that reduces pressure quickly;
- a fundamental solution that would reduce the underlying cause;
- a delay before fundamental benefits appear;
- reduced attention or investment in the fundamental solution;
- growing dependency on symptomatic relief;
- burden shifted to groups, institutions, ecosystems, or future time periods outside the dominant metric boundary.

## Core questions

1. What recurring symptom creates pressure?
2. What symptomatic solution provides relief?
3. What fundamental solution is delayed or displaced?
4. Is reliance on the symptomatic solution increasing?
5. Is fundamental capacity improving or eroding?
6. Who carries the burden of the unresolved problem?
7. What metrics reward relief but ignore repair?
8. What transition path would reduce dependency?
