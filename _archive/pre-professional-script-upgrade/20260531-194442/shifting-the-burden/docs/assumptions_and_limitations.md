# Assumptions and Limitations

## Assumptions

- Synthetic examples represent stylized system behavior.
- Variables are conceptual proxies, not empirical measurements.
- A higher dependency indicator means the system relies more heavily on symptomatic relief relative to repair and capacity.
- Externalized burden includes costs not counted inside narrow institutional metrics.

## Limitations

- These examples do not estimate real-world policy effects.
- Model parameters are illustrative.
- Real systems require participatory diagnosis, domain expertise, historical context, and empirical calibration.
- The framework should not be used to make automated decisions about people, benefits, employment, care, or eligibility.
