# Responsible Use

This repository is for systems learning, public-interest analysis, and reproducible methods demonstrations. Do not use these examples for individual ranking, employment selection, student tracking, credit decisions, eligibility scoring, surveillance, or punitive decision-making.

Responsible use requires:

- transparent assumptions;
- aggregate and structural interpretation;
- distributional analysis;
- historical and institutional context;
- human review and participatory governance;
- safeguards against stigmatizing under-resourced groups.
