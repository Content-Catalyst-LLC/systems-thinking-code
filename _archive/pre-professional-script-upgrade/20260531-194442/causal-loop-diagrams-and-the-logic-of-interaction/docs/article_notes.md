# Article Notes

This scaffold supports an article on causal loop diagrams and the logic of interaction. The central claim is that causal-loop diagrams are useful when they clarify relationships, feedback, delays, polarity, boundary choices, and behavior-over-time patterns.

Key article concepts:

- variables as quantities that can change;
- arrows as causal claims;
- positive and negative link polarity;
- reinforcing and balancing loop polarity;
- delays as timing gaps;
- behavior over time as the test of structure;
- boundaries as ethical and analytical choices;
- diagrams as contestable models rather than decorative maps.
