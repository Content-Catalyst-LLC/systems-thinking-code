# Modeling Principles

The fixes-that-fail archetype should be modeled with both the visible relief loop and the delayed consequence loop. A model that stops when the symptom improves will overstate success.

Core modeling principles:

- Track the problem symptom over time.
- Represent the quick fix and its immediate relief.
- Represent delayed side effects explicitly.
- Track capacity, trust, infrastructure condition, ecological resilience, or other supporting stocks.
- Compare quick-fix-only scenarios with relief-plus-repair scenarios.
- Include distributional effects: who gets relief and who bears delayed cost.
