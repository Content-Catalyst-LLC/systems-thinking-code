# Diagnostic Questions

- What visible symptom creates pressure?
- What quick fix reduces the symptom?
- What immediate relief does the fix provide?
- What delayed consequence does the fix create?
- What supporting stock is depleted?
- Who receives credit for the fix?
- Who bears the delayed cost?
- What fundamental solution is being delayed?
- Is reliance on the fix increasing over time?
- What transition criteria would reduce dependence on the fix?
