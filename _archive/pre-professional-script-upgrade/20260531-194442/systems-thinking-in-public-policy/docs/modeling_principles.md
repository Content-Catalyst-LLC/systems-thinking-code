# Modeling Principles

1. Model policy behavior over time, not only policy intent or legal text.
2. Treat administrative burden, public trust, capacity, feedback, and distribution as core policy variables.
3. Distinguish pressure responses from structural redesign.
4. Preserve group-level results rather than hiding burden in aggregate averages.
5. Validate data before using outputs for analysis.
6. Document assumptions, boundaries, ethical risks, and limitations.
7. Use models to support public learning, not to replace democratic judgment or community knowledge.
