# Overshoot Correction Framework

1. Identify the pressure that is growing.
2. Identify the stock, buffer, or capacity being depleted.
3. Define the safe operating limit and warning threshold.
4. Locate delayed feedback.
5. Compare prevention, moderate correction, and late crisis repair.
6. Evaluate who benefits from overshoot and who bears collapse costs.
7. Redesign goals, incentives, and feedback so limits become visible and actionable earlier.
