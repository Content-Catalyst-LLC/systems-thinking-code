# Modeling Principles

- Treat overshoot as a dynamic relationship among pressure, limits, stocks, buffers, feedback delay, and correction capacity.
- Model stocks explicitly when possible: trust, workforce capacity, maintenance condition, ecological resilience, debt, legitimacy, and buffers.
- Separate safe operating limits from warning thresholds and collapse thresholds.
- Compare early prevention, late correction, and emergency repair scenarios.
- Interpret model output alongside institutional, ethical, ecological, and stakeholder evidence.
