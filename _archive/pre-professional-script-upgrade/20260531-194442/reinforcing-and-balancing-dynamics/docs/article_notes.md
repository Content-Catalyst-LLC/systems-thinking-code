# Article Notes

Article: Reinforcing and Balancing Dynamics

Core concepts:

- reinforcing dynamics amplify change
- balancing dynamics counteract change
- loop interaction shapes system behavior
- limits to growth emerge when reinforcing growth meets constraint
- success-to-the-successful dynamics can compound advantage
- delayed balancing loops can create oscillation and overcorrection
- feedback structures raise ethical questions about what systems reward, suppress, stabilize, or ignore
