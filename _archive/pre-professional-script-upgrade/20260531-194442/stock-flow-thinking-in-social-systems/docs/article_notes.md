# Article Notes

Article: Stock-Flow Thinking in Social Systems

Core argument: social systems store history through accumulated stocks such as trust, wealth, debt, burden, capability, legitimacy, institutional capacity, social cohesion, and resilience. Social repair requires changing the flows that build or drain those stocks.

Key sections:

- Why stock-flow thinking matters in social systems
- Social stocks and social flows
- History as accumulation
- Trust, legitimacy, and repair
- Wealth, debt, and inequality
- Administrative burden as a stock
- Capability and social development
- Feedback and path dependence
- Policy and repair
- Ethics and unequal accumulation
