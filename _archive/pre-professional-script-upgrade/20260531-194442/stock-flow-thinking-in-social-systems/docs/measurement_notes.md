# Measurement Notes

Many important social stocks are difficult to measure directly. Quantitative indicators should be used with caution and should be paired with qualitative evidence, historical context, participatory knowledge, and ethical interpretation.

## Measurement Risks

- Treating proxies as the real condition
- Hiding unequal accumulation behind averages
- Counting services delivered rather than stock change
- Measuring agency workload while ignoring public burden
- Ignoring people who exit processes before being counted
- Treating distrust as attitude rather than accumulated experience

## Suggested Evidence Mix

- Administrative data
- Survey data
- Interviews
- Participatory mapping
- Historical analysis
- Time-use evidence
- Complaint, appeal, and exit data
- Distributional and place-based analysis
