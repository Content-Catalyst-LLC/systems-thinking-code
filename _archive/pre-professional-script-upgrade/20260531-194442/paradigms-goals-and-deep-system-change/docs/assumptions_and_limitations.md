# Assumptions and Limitations

This scaffold uses synthetic data and simplified models. The examples are not predictive and should not be used for operational decisions. They are designed to support article readers in thinking about goals, paradigms, objective functions, and boundary choices.

Limitations:

- Social concepts such as dignity, trust, burden, and harm cannot be fully reduced to simple metrics.
- Objective functions can clarify priorities but can also hide values if presented as neutral.
- Boundary expansion requires participatory evidence and domain knowledge.
- Real-world use requires governance, validation, accountability, and ethical review.
