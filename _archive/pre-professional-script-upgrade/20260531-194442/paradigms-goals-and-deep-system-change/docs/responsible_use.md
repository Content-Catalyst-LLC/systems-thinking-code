# Responsible Use

This repository is for synthetic-data education and systems-thinking demonstration. It should not be used to automate high-stakes decisions or justify institutional action without participatory review, domain expertise, data governance, uncertainty analysis, legal review, and accountability mechanisms.

Do not use these examples for:

- employment screening or workplace discipline;
- benefits eligibility decisions;
- policing or surveillance;
- credit, insurance, or healthcare determinations;
- automated public-service denial;
- ranking people or communities for punitive intervention.

Appropriate uses include learning, transparent scenario exploration, article support, and discussion of how goals and paradigms shape systems.
